belessfat

Support for the belessfat iOS app — a free, local-first calorie, macro, and progress tracker.

Contact

Need help, found a bug, or have a feature request? Email nathanhherr@gmail.com or open an issue on GitHub.

App notes

  • belessfat is fully free — no subscriptions and no features behind a paywall.
  • Your diary, weight, workouts, and progress photos stay on your device.
  • Optional AI features use your own API key (BYOK). Keys are stored in the iOS Keychain.
  • HealthKit access is optional and only used for steps/activity you authorize.
