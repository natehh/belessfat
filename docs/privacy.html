Overview

belessfat (“the App”) is a personal calorie, macro, workout, and progress tracking app. It is designed to keep your data on your device. This policy explains what information the App handles and when anything may leave your device.

Data stored on your device

The App stores the following locally on your iPhone:

  • Food diary entries, custom foods, and recipes
  • Weight entries and weight goals
  • Workout logs
  • Progress photos and optional body-fat estimates tied to those photos
  • App settings (including body profile fields used for estimates)
  • Optional AI API keys in the iOS Keychain

We do not operate a belessfat account system, and we do not host your diary on our servers.

HealthKit

If you grant permission, the App may read steps and active energy from Apple Health, and may write workout-related data you choose to sync. You can revoke HealthKit access in iOS Settings at any time.

Camera and photos

Camera and photo library access are used for features you choose, such as barcode/label scanning, food photo estimates, and progress photos. Progress photos are stored in the App’s local storage on your device.

Optional AI features

AI food estimates, recipe import assistance, and body-fat estimates are optional. When you run an in-app AI estimate with your own API key, the relevant content (for example a food photo, recipe text, or progress photos plus profile context you provide) is sent to the AI provider you configured (such as OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, or Cursor). That provider’s privacy policy then applies to that request.

If you use the clipboard/prompt flow instead, nothing is sent by the App to an AI provider; you paste the prompt into an AI app of your choice.

Network services

Food lookup features may contact third-party nutrition databases (for example USDA or Open Food Facts) when you search for foods. Those services receive your search query.

Analytics and advertising

The App does not include third-party advertising SDKs and does not sell personal data. We do not use your diary contents for advertising.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • You can delete diary items, photos, and other data inside the App.
  • You can remove AI API keys in Settings.
  • You can delete the App to remove local App data from your device.
  • You can manage HealthKit, camera, and photo permissions in iOS Settings.
